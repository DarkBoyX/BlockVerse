Publishing bumps the live version. Anyone currently in the game gets pulled out immediately with a "please refresh" message; anyone just browsing the site gets the same notice next time they interact with it.

Accounts -

Kick pulls someone out of Baseplate only. Ban blocks login and logs them out everywhere immediately. Terminate permanently deletes the account and unfriends it from everyone. None of this is undoable except Ban (you can unban from here or from the dedicated Banned tab).

Banned accounts -

Unban clears the ban immediately and lets them log back in right away.

Requested Games -

Accept publishes the game (icon + banner) to the home page for everyone. Deny sends the reason back to the creator so they can fix it up and resubmit.

Published Games -

Shutdown pulls a live game off the home page immediately - the creator can resubmit it any time from Studio.
